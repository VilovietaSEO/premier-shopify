      1. Executive Summary

      Redesign and rebuild of independencephone.com as a single-domain Shopify site — direct to Shopify, no WordPress
      interim. The new site is a Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)-optimized engine for the Independence Phone line
      (Freedom $100 + Patriot $150, $17.76/month service) with a 250th-Anniversary brand narrative, a Search Engine
      Optimization (SEO) foundation for organic discovery, an AI Optimization (AIO) foundation for visibility in ChatGPT /
      Claude / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews, and page-speed + image optimization targeting sub-3-second Largest
      Contentful Paint (LCP) on 4G mobile.

      Three pages, all custom-designed:

        1. Home — the main conversion page
        2. Category — the collection page that holds Freedom and Patriot
        3. Product — the product page template that serves both Stock Keeping Units (SKUs)

      Built so Jordan can run it without a developer — page copy, product photos, FAQs, and prices are all editable through
      Shopify's admin. Includes a written guide and recorded walkthrough video.

      Fixed price: $11,000 USD · family & friends rate: $5,500 (50% off) for the same scope. Delivery: 2 weeks from
      kickoff, gated on Jordan's inputs in §8.
