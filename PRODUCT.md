# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents and family decision-makers who want a first phone or family contact device without introducing apps, browsers, social feeds, or a full smartphone. They are comparing practical reachability needs: school days, short home-alone windows, grandparents, and family coordination.

## Product Purpose

Patriot Phone is a Shopify storefront for a simpler family phone offer. Success means visitors quickly understand the core promise, click Order Now, choose between Classic Phone and Rugged Phone on the purchase page, understand service and add-ons, and contact or purchase without unsupported feature assumptions.

## Brand Personality

Practical, protective, trustworthy. The tone should feel parent-first and family-safe, with patriotism supporting trust rather than leading the pitch.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a gadget hype page, a political campaign site, a military surplus store, or a dense telecom pricing page. Avoid heavy red-white-blue theming, dark patriotic drama, generic AI landing-page card grids, and copy that repeats the same "no apps, no browser" claim in every section.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the parent job: reachable kids, fewer negotiations, less internet exposure.
- Keep patriotism as a trust layer, not the product's first argument.
- Make the choice simple: two phones, clear service, optional add-ons.
- Prefer calm confidence over urgency or hype.
- Do not imply unsupported features such as texting, GPS, camera, cellular mobility, apps, browser access, or emergency calling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Maintain readable contrast, large touch targets, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and plain-language copy for parents scanning on mobile.
